**Exp-7.py (HMM POS Tagging)**:

3. Gaining experience with handling unknown words and probability calculations in NLP applications

**Exp-8.py (Viterbi POS Tagging)**:
1. Understanding how the Viterbi algorithm efficiently finds the most likely sequence of POS tags
2. Learning to implement dynamic programming solutions for sequence labeling problems
3. Gaining practical experience with backtracking algorithms and probability-based decision making in NLP

Here's a concise 3-point theory for each experiment:

**Exp-6.py (Text Chunking)**:
1. Implements text chunking using NLTK to group words into meaningful phrases based on their POS tags
2. Uses RegexpParser with grammar rules to identify noun phrases (NP), verb phrases (VP), and prepositional phrases (PP)
3. Visualizes the hierarchical structure of chunks in a parse tree format

**Exp-7.py (HMM POS Tagging)**:
1. Implements Part-of-Speech tagging using Hidden Markov Model with transition and emission probabilities
2. Uses the Brown corpus to train the model by calculating tag-to-tag and word-to-tag probabilities
3. Predicts POS tags for new sentences using probability matrices and dynamic programming

**Exp-8.py (Viterbi POS Tagging)**:
1. Implements POS tagging using the Viterbi algorithm to find the most likely sequence of tags
2. Uses backpointing to trace the optimal path through tag sequences
3. Handles unknown words through smoothing and calculates probabilities from training data
